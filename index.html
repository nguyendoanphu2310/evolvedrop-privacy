Privacy Policy — Evolve Drop

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Evolve Drop ("the app") is developed by TakanProduct.

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.

  • The app works fully offline and does not require an account.
  • Game progress (best scores and unlocked creatures) is stored only on your device, using local app storage. It never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or files.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
